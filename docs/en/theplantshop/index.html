The Plant Shop is a cozy horror game where you manage a small plant shop. Craft plants, serve late-night customers and grow your plant collection to attract more people.

Game Info

  • Status: In Development
  • Release: 2026
  • Genre: Horror, Management, Adventure, Casual
  • Languages: Spanish, English, German, French, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Chinese (Simplified), Korean, Russian
  • Platform: Windows

    • MANAGE A PLANT SHOP

    You play as Silvia, a 26 years old girl who decides to open her own small plant shop on a late-night shift because she has a corporate job during the day.

    ATTEND CUSTOMERS

    Your job is to serve the customers coming to the shop. Depending on the order you will have to craft flowers, plants or cacti.

    CRAFT PLANTS

    Crafting plants is easy. You just need a plant pot, soil, a seed and water.

    PSYCHOLOGICAL HORROR

    The customers talk about a curse that struck the town 30 years ago. As you manage your shop, you begin to see and hear strange things. Is the curse real—or just an old legend?
